# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters screening candidates for full-time engineering roles. They skim for titles, scope, and results before deciding whether to read further. (Engineers and interviewers vetting depth, and prospective clients or partners, were offered and not selected as primary.)

## Product Purpose

marich.dev is Andrew Marich's personal software portfolio. It presents his current and past engineering work accurately and makes a hiring decision easy. Success was not stated beyond this; do not invent a conversion goal.

## Positioning

Andrew builds products and leads the engineering behind them: Co-Founder & CTO of Balance Health (sole engineer on a patient-billing platform) and, until October 2026, Lead Software Engineer at B1 Marketing Group (a legal-claimant qualification platform through its SaaS launch).

## Operating Context

- Static Astro site with React islands, served from Cloudflare at marich.dev.
- Content mirrors a frozen resume that lives in a separate private repo; the site is updated from the resume, not the reverse.
- Changes go through pull requests on `andrewmarich/software-portfolio`; merging to `main` deploys.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **The resume is the source of truth for every public claim:** roles, dates, titles, metrics, and skills. Ask before adding or changing a claim.
- **The playful layers stay:** boot sequence, hidden terminal, Konami code, `/secret` page, and achievements are a deliberate part of the site.
- Terminology: "full-stack engineer" is the hero headline; resume titles are Software Engineer, Lead Software Engineer, and Co-Founder & CTO.
- Undecided: whether the B1 project card's screenshot (B1 branding, test data) should stay. It currently does by owner decision, and the PR flags it for review. Employer-internal detail was offered as a constraint and not selected, so no blanket rule is recorded.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Andrew Marich. Contact: andrew@marich.dev, github.com/andrewmarich, linkedin.com/in/andrewmarich.
- Hero line (approved): "I build products and lead the engineering behind them."

## Evidence on Hand

- The frozen resume and Career Source Record (private repo) back all claims.
- Project screenshots: B1 admin dashboard (test data) and Balance dashboard (synthetic demo practice on staging, seeded by `apps/web/scripts/seed-demo-practice.ts` in the Balance repo).
- Absent, and not to be fabricated: testimonials, customer logos, press, case-study write-ups, public code links for the B1 or Balance products.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim traces to the resume.
2. Lead with ownership and results, not technology lists.
3. A recruiter should understand who Andrew is and what he has led within seconds of arriving.
4. Personality lives in discoverable layers and never gets in the way of scanning.
